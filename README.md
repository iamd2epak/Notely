# Notely

A clean, private notes app for Android. Everything stays on your phone.

## Features

- Rich text: bold, headings, checklists, lists, colours and highlights
- Topics with colours, pinned notes, and search
- Grid or list view, with drag & drop to reorder
- Select and delete many notes at once
- Light, dark or system theme
- Backup and restore, with optional password encryption

## Why it's different

- **No account, no server, no tracking.** Your notes never leave your phone.
- **Truly locked notes.** Locked notes are encrypted (AES-256), not just hidden behind a screen.
- **Fast and simple.** It stays smooth with 1000+ notes and has no clutter.
